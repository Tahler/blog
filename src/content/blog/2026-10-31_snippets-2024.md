## 2024<a name="2024"></a>

- What is the pain you want to sustain? You can't have a pain-free life, so choose the pain that reaps the rewards you want.\
  https://markmanson.net/question
- Do as much as you can with friends of friends -- hiring, dating, finding new friends -- it's almost always good\
  https://x.com/nickcammarata/status/1804934208844157171
- One of the great mistakes in life is suffering for years because you didn't want to feel foolish for five minutes. You don't want to apologize, so you let a relationship deteriorate. You're scared of the sting of rejection, so you don't ask for what you want. You fear people will say your idea is dumb, so you never start the business. Nobody likes feeling foolish, but the feeling fades quickly. The willingness to endure five minutes of discomfort turns out to be a meaningful dividing line in life.
- What might this look like if it were easy / fun?
- Excitement is the more practical synonym for happiness, and it is precisely what you should strive to chase. It is the cure-all. The question is not "what do I want?" or "what are my goals?" but "What would excite me?"
