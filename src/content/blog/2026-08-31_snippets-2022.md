Here's the next throwback batch of snippets, but let me highlight just a few.

2022 was the lead up to ChatGPT, so looking back from now, in "the year of agents", it's funny to see this hyper-relevant note:

> Software engineers are there to solve ambiguities and make computers do the rest.

These notes I still often quote or think about, so it was fun to rediscover their sources:

> - Prioritization is efficiency.
> - If you are rich and tall, your brother is more likely to also be rich than also tall.
> - If you want to bond with someone, start a recurring event like "coffee Tuesdays".
> - Most people don't actually mind being asked "sensitive" questions.
> - Imitate, then innovate.
> - Most social media content is banal and correct (Comet Ping Pong is a restaurant with pretty good pizza), but algorithms amplify what is shared, and only insane content is shared (Comet Ping Pong is the secret headquarters of a cabal of child molesters).

And lastly, one I wish occurred to me more often: 

> Aside from my thoughts, am I okay?

As before, there's navigation between headings and stars on promoted notes. You can read just the top five or dive deeper. 

I'll back-publish 2023-2026 in the remaining four months this year.

Enjoy!
