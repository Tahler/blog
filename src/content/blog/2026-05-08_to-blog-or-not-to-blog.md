---

I've hesitated to make a personal site like this for years. But in quitting my job, I want a place to share what I'm building and learning.

I did consider other platforms. X is great for sharing projects and [I did create one](https://x.com/ber_tyl), but it's sometimes too politically charged. And not everyone has one. Instagram isn't quite right for this type of content. Or for me. Somehow everytime I open it I'm scrolling reels. But _almost_ everyone has one.

Email feels better. Everyone has at least one. [bearblog.dev](https://bearblog.dev) is awesome, but it doesn't offer tag-based subscriptions and requires Buttondown-branded emails. Substack has some amazing writers (s/o [Uncharted Territories](https://unchartedterritories.tomaspueyo.com/) in particular) and _does_ have email notifications. Both are low-friction but both are still platforms. I want something more personal.

Because I'm not aspiring to share with the world, I'm sharing with you.
